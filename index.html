(03)

Two components do most of the work.

Processing

HD Noise Cancelling
Processor QN3

  • Seven times faster than the QN12
  • Twelve microphones, tuned in real time3
  • Look-ahead noise shaper

The QN3 re-reads the twelve microphones continuously and adjusts cancellation against what it hears rather than against a stored profile. Its look-ahead noise shaper runs during digital-to-analogue conversion, before the signal reaches the driver.

Transducer

30 mm carbon fibre
composite driver

  • High-rigidity carbon fibre composite dome
  • 4 Hz – 40,000 Hz, wired4
  • Purpose-built voice coil structure

A stiffer dome moves as one piece for longer before it starts to flex, which is what keeps the top of the range from smearing. One driver per earcup — the ramp on the diagram above is this part.

(05)

Across the surface.

Control surface

The whole outer face of the right cup is the control. Swipe vertically for volume, horizontally to skip, hold to switch between ambient sound and full cancellation. Speak-to-Chat drops the volume and opens the microphones the moment you start talking.

Microphone array

Twelve microphones sit around the rim of both cups.3 Six of them — marked here — form the beamforming array that isolates your voice on a call. The remaining six feed the QN3's cancellation loop.

(06)

What the processing is for.

  • Adaptive NC Optimizer

    Re-measures the seal, the shape of your head and the air pressure around you, and re-tunes cancellation continuously rather than at pairing.

  • Scene-based Listening

    Reads what you are listening to and shifts the spatial treatment to suit it — new on this model.

  • 360 Reality Audio

    Object-based spatial audio on supported services, mapped to your own ear photographs.

  • 360 Upmix for Cinema

    Lifts ordinary stereo film soundtracks into the same spatial field.

  • DSEE Extreme

    Restores the high-frequency detail that compression discards, in real time.

  • Adaptive Sound Control

    Learns the places you go and changes ambient settings when you arrive.

  • Speak-to-Chat

    Pauses playback and opens the microphones when you begin to speak.

  • Multipoint and Auto Switch

    Two devices connected at once, with the audio following whichever one is playing.

Footnotes

1. Sony describes the WH-1000XM6 as offering “the best noise cancellation”. Sony states the method as ambient noise reduction based on IEC-compliant guidelines, measured against the top ten brands by market share. The competitor set is not named by Sony. Source: Sony US press release, 15 May 2025.

2. Sony’s US press release states the QN3 is “seven times faster than the previous model”. That release does not name the baseline chip; Sony’s European press copy and press-briefed reporting from Engadget and Notebookcheck both identify it as the QN1 used in the WH-1000XM5. The 7.0× figure is Sony’s; the QN1 attribution is those secondary sources.

3. Twelve microphones in total, which Sony states is “1.5 times more than the WH-1000XM5” — a baseline of eight. Six form the AI-based beamforming array used for voice calls. Source: Sony.

4. 4 Hz – 40,000 Hz measured wired to IEC method. 20 Hz – 40,000 Hz over Bluetooth using LDAC at 96 kHz / 990 kbps. Source: Sony specification.

5. Sony’s published operating times: up to 30 hours with noise cancelling on using AAC, SBC or LC3; up to 26 hours using LDAC; up to 40 hours with noise cancelling off. Actual time varies with volume, content and ambient temperature. Source: Sony Help Guide.

6. Three minutes of charging returns approximately three hours of playback using a commercially available USB PD adaptor at 9 V / 2.3 A. The same three minutes returns approximately one hour using a standard 5 V / 1.5 A supply, which is the baseline the 3.0× figure is measured from. Sony states the test conditions as DSEE Extreme and EQ off, AAC codec. Source: Sony Help Guide.

7. Approximately 254 g. Source: Sony Help Guide specification.

8. $449.99 USD at launch, announced 15 May 2025. Source: Sony US press release. At least one review outlet lists a $459.99 MSRP; the launch figure is used here because it is the one Sony published.
